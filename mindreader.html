The Mind Reader

One expression. It already knows your answer.

Think of any number. Don't tell anyone. Type it here — the spirits are listening.

✿

Your number walks in, gets doubled, padded, halved — and cancels itself out. Only the 5 survives. And it says 5.0, not 5, because / always produces a float.

magic = (n * 2 + 10) / 2 - n   # always 5.0
